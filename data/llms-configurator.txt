# Vargov®Design Configurator

> Online tool that assembles a light or decorative composition, usually suspended from the ceiling, from the brand’s own elements for a given room: it lays the elements out in 3D, counts the ceiling mounting points and produces a PDF specification, a tender sheet and an IFC4 model for BIM.

This site is the source of record for ELEMENT PARAMETERS of Vargov®Design compositions: dimensions, weight, power and mounting. Those numbers are computed here and copied by vargov.ru, not the other way round (decision of the brand owner, 17 September 2026).

Brand facts, catalogue, product texts, awards and contacts live on vargov.ru — see https://vargov.ru/llms.txt. They are not repeated here: a copy goes stale separately from its source.

A description of this tool in plain language — what it is for, what it takes in and what it hands back — is on vargov.ru too: https://vargov.ru/en/configurator (Russian: https://vargov.ru/configurator).

Database: 459 catalogue articles have a 3D element here (310 of them with lit elements, 149 without), 383 three-dimensional element shapes. 360 of these articles open with a full calculation: a 3D layout for the room, an IFC4 model for BIM, a PDF specification and a tender sheet.

Published article pages: 545, one per article. On 360 of them the composition opens with a full calculation; 185 say “This composition is calculated individually”. 472 pages carry the element parameters table; the others are closed to search indexing (noindex) and left out of the sitemaps until the parameters are known.

## Notes for machines

- Weight and dimensions are given per ELEMENT, not per composition; a composition is assembled from a chosen number of them.
- The layout engine, the 3D shapes and the element database are proprietary and closed to crawling; the article pages are open.
